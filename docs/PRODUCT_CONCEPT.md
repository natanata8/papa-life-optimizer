# PRODUCT_CONCEPT — Papa Life Optimizer

## One-sentence concept
Papa Life Optimizer is a household-operations AI that starts from a father's "I want to..." event, predicts the hidden household impact it creates, and helps absorb that impact without cancelling his personal time.

Short product explanation:
> やりたい予定から、家庭の負担を先回りして整える。

Brand line:
> 自分の時間も、家族の余裕も。

Brand principle:
> 責めない。止めない。先回りする。

## Problem

A calendar event is not the same as its household impact.

"土曜18時から飲み会" is one event in a calendar, but inside the household it can create:
- childcare tasks
- meal preparation
- bath / bedtime
- mental load
- time constraints
- coordination work
- recovery work the next day

The father often cannot see the full chain in advance. As a result:
- every personal event becomes a new negotiation
- the partner must explain hidden work repeatedly
- goodwill does not translate into the right preparation
- freedom and responsibility are framed as opposites

## Primary user
Primary user: fathers raising children.

More specifically:
> A father who wants personal time, work time, hobbies or rest, but does not want to externalize the resulting household burden onto his partner.

Initial buyer: father.
Secondary user / beneficiary: partner.
Beneficiary: father, partner, children and household as a whole.

The MVP must create partner-side value without requiring a partner account or partner data entry.

## Core Job To Be Done
> 自分の予定を諦めずに、家庭に何が残るかを先に理解して、必要なことだけ整えてから動きたい。

## Product promise
> やりたいことを諦めるのではなく、行ける形に家庭を整える。

## Core mechanism
Personal Event
→ Household Impact Prediction
→ Load Absorption
→ Event
→ Recovery
→ Learning

The product predicts what the event will create:
- work
- childcare
- cognitive load
- time constraints
- coordination load
- recovery debt
- fatigue / risk
- partner personal-time loss

Then it proposes the smallest set of high-impact actions.

## Optimization target
Primary internal target: Household Buffer.

Definition:
> The household's remaining capacity to absorb schedule changes, fatigue, childcare disruptions and unexpected events without chronically pushing load onto the same person.

User-facing optimization should not expose an abstract household score by default.

Secondary outcomes:
- Papa Free Time
- Partner Free Time
- Task Debt
- Cognitive Load transferred
- Coordination Cost
- Recovery Load

Do not optimize for daily 50:50 equality.
Optimize against chronic load accumulation on one person.

## North Star
Balanced Freedom Event Rate

> The percentage of desired personal events completed without causing major additional household burden or unresolved recovery debt.

MVP proxy metrics:
- recommendation execution
- desired event completion
- repeat use
- partner-side perceived helpfulness when available

## MVP scope
Initial scenarios:
1. 飲み会
2. 休日朝寝坊
3. ゴルフ

MVP flow:
Event input
→ 2–4 contextual questions
→ Household Impact Preview
→ 3–5 concrete BEFORE / RECOVERY actions
→ execution check
→ learn for next time

Not required for MVP:
- partner account
- calendar sync
- household fairness score
- points / gamification
- free-time balance ledger
- complete household task database
- large notification system
- long-form AI chat
- subscription implementation

## Product boundaries
We are NOT:
- a chore tracker
- a household fairness scoring app
- a parenting encyclopedia
- a "wife攻略" tool
- a good-husband diagnosis
- a therapy app
- a couple surveillance tool
- a household 50:50 calculator
- a service that corrects fathers
- a generic family organizer

## AI boundary
AI may:
- predict likely tasks and time constraints
- suggest preparation and recovery actions
- compare with known household patterns
- surface factual historical patterns

AI must not:
- diagnose the partner's feelings
- decide who is right
- judge love, fairness or relationship quality
- label the father as selfish / irresponsible
- label the partner as unreasonable
- assign an "unfairness score"

Facts may be shown. Moral judgments must not be automated.

## Expansion logic
Do not begin by building a full household OS.

Win first at:
> Personal Event → Household Impact

Only after this is validated may the product expand toward:
Event Optimizer
→ Household Buffer Engine
→ Family Operating Layer
