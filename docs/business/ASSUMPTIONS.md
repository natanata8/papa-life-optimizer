# ASSUMPTIONS — Papa Life Optimizer

This file tracks important business hypotheses separately from decisions.

## A-001 — Core customer pain

**Hypothesis**
A meaningful initial customer segment is fathers who believe they already contribute to work, childcare, and household tasks, yet are still frequently told they are not anticipating or owning enough of the household load.

**Origin**
Human.

**Status**
`HUMAN_HYPOTHESIS`

**Evidence so far**
Product concept and Human-provided examples only. No formal interview validation recorded here yet.

**Validation**
Interview approximately 5 people who resemble the initial target.

**Success condition**
At least 3/5 independently describe a recent concrete episode where they believed they were contributing substantially but still experienced conflict, criticism, or confusion about what was expected.

**Failure condition**
The problem is weak, rare, or consistently secondary to a different problem.

## A-002 — Desire-first entry

**Hypothesis**
Starting with "何したい？" and a desired real-life event produces stronger engagement than starting with chores, fairness, or household duties.

**Origin**
Human + current product thesis.

**Status**
`HUMAN_HYPOTHESIS`

**Validation**
Prototype test using initial scenarios such as drinking, sleeping in, or golf.

**Success condition**
Users can state a real event naturally, understand the resulting household-impact preview, and prefer this entry over a generic household-management flow.

**Failure condition**
Users need a different entry point before they can understand the product value.
