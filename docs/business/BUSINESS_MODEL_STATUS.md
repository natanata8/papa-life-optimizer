# BUSINESS_MODEL_STATUS — Papa Life Optimizer

This document summarizes the current business model without presenting hypotheses as settled facts.

## 1. Customer / Payer

### `DECIDED`
- Initial payer candidate is the father himself.
- The product must create partner-side value without requiring a partner account for MVP.
- The product is not a "wife攻略" service.

### `HUMAN_HYPOTHESIS`
- Strong initial segment: fathers who feel they already work, do childcare and household tasks, yet are still criticized for not anticipating or owning enough of the household load.
- Initial life stage likely centers on pre-parenthood through families with young children.

### `UNKNOWN`
- Exact age band.
- Whether dual-income vs single-income households should be segmented.
- Whether eventual payer should expand to family plans, employers, or benefits programs.

## 2. Pain / Desire

### `HUMAN_HYPOTHESIS`
Pain:
- "I am already doing a lot, but I still get told I am not thinking ahead."
- personal time has collapsed
- work events, drinking, golf, rest, or overtime create household friction
- both partners can feel "I am the one carrying this"

Desire:
- keep personal time without simply transferring the cost to the partner
- know what to do before a desired event
- reduce household friction and create more margin for both adults

## 3. Offer

### `DECIDED`
Core mechanism:
> Personal Event → Household Impact → Load Absorption

Preferred entry:
> 何したい？

Initial scenarios in current canonical concept:
- 飲み会
- 休日朝寝坊
- ゴルフ

The service should recommend the smallest useful set of high-impact actions rather than maximize task count.

### `HUMAN_HYPOTHESIS`
Post-MVP value may include:
- household memory / personalization
- reminders
- broader day / week optimization
- contextual AI chat
- scheduling support

## 4. Price

### `UNKNOWN`
No canonical price is decided.

Previously discussed numbers are test candidates only and must not be treated as approved pricing.

Validation must eventually use actual displayed prices and, when ready, real paid conversion.

## 5. Acquisition

### `HUMAN_HYPOTHESIS`
Potential channels:
- SEO around real problem language
- Instagram / TikTok / X
- diagnostic content
- interviews / original research content
- selected offline opportunities

These are candidates, not a committed multi-channel strategy.

### `UNKNOWN`
- first channel to scale
- CAC
- channel-quality differences
- whether partner-side sharing becomes a meaningful acquisition loop

## 6. Revenue Funnel

### `HUMAN_HYPOTHESIS`
Likely primary path:
Awareness
→ self-recognition
→ free first-use / diagnosis
→ real-life event optimization
→ repeat use
→ persistent memory / reminders / personalization
→ paid subscription

Potential secondary revenue sources such as affiliate, B2B benefits, sponsorship, or advertising remain later-stage options unless explicitly prioritized.

## 7. Business Goal

### `DECIDED`
Current phase is not MRR maximization.

The business must first prove:
1. users repeatedly use the product in real life
2. enough users perceive sufficient value to pay

## 8. North Star / validation KPI

### `DECIDED`
Canonical product North Star remains:
> Balanced Freedom Event Rate

See `docs/PRODUCT_CONCEPT.md`.

### `HUMAN_HYPOTHESIS`
For the present validation phase, a simpler behavioral metric is more useful:
> 30-day second-use rate

Definition:
> Percentage of users who use Papa Life Optimizer for a second real-life event within 30 days of first use.

No target threshold is decided yet.

## Next validation priority

1. Validate the core customer pain through interviews.
2. Validate desire-first event entry.
3. Validate second use in real life.
4. Only then harden price and acquisition strategy.

Do not polish unresolved assumptions into final strategy language.
