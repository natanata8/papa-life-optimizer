## Required exploration directions

### A. Life Operations
Treat the event as an operational sequence.

Explore:
- event timeline
- impact map
- before / absence / recovery
- household buffer
- small next-action plan
- contingency / risk visibility

Must not become:
- enterprise dashboard
- household control center
- dense KPI surface

Goal:
Make invisible household consequences understandable at a glance.

### B. Editorial Decision Assistant
Conversation and editorial sequencing lead the experience.

Example:
> 土曜、ゴルフに行きたい。
> いいと思います。
> この3つだけ先に整えましょう。

Explore:
- strong editorial hierarchy
- conversational copy
- narrative sequence
- minimal containers
- calm adult visual language
- progressive disclosure

Goal:
Make the product feel like an intelligent adult advisor, not a task manager.

### C. Desire-first Personal Utility
Make the user's desire visually dominant.

Example:
> 飲みに行きたい。

Then quietly reveal:
- impact
- preparation
- recovery

Explore:
- bold but mature typography
- restrained accent
- clear transition from desire → consequence → action
- minimal controls
- strong single-purpose screens

Goal:
Create category distinction: a household product that starts from the father's actual desire.

## Common requirements
Every direction must:
- start from the user's desire
- avoid moral judgment
- avoid wife/partner-as-enemy framing
- avoid permission language
- show only 3–5 high-impact actions
- preserve adult / practical / calm character
- work on mobile
- avoid generic SaaS
- avoid parenting-app aesthetics
- avoid cute illustration
- avoid gamification
- avoid fairness scores
- avoid partner mood scores
- avoid chore dashboards
- avoid repetitive cards

## Scenario content
Use MVP scenarios:
1. 飲み会
2. 休日朝寝坊
3. ゴルフ

At least one direction should demonstrate each scenario across the exploration set.

## Comparison criteria
Compare:
- Desire First clarity
- Preparation-not-Permission
- household impact comprehension
- actionability
- adult brand fit
- distinctiveness
- low-friction interaction
- mobile resilience
- accessibility
- implementation feasibility
- AI-slop risk

Do not pick a winner automatically.

After exploration:
- document strengths / weaknesses
- identify reusable elements
- propose a synthesis
- hand decisions to Human
- formalize approved result in Figma
